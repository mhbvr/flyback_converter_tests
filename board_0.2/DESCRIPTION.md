# Components
external pwm generator 3.3 V connected to mcp1407. mcp1407 connected to voltage source 5V. between powera and groung of mcp1407 100nF ceramic and 10uF electrolityc capacitors. output of mcp1407 connected to gate of CSD18533KCS mosfet. mosfet on the low side of primary of DA2033-ALD. secondary is connected to qh05tz600 diod and 2.2uF film capacitor and load resistor in paralell. There is no other components at all.
